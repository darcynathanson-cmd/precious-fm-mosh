# MOSH — Precious FM datamosh tool

A self-contained, in-browser datamosh / glitch studio. No install, nothing uploaded — everything runs locally in your browser and your images stay on your device.

**Live tool:** once GitHub Pages is on, it's at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`

## What it does

- Load a **photo, GIF, or video** and glitch it in real time.
- Looks: **blend / melt / streak / chaos / random**, with intensity, block-size and keep-original sliders.
- Apply it to the **whole image**, to **sections**, or to a **typed word** (the letters become a crisp window over the glitch), with a dozen fonts.
- **Colour FX** (hue / saturation / contrast / invert), **glitch FX** (pixel sort, RGB time-displacement, feedback trails), and a **Fairlight CVI '86** mode (pixelate, posterize, colour palettes, mirror, colour-cycling).
- **Beat sync** — load a track and the glitch pulses to the bass; the music is baked into recordings.
- Optional **two-clip mosh** — bloom one clip into another for a transition.
- **Auto-move** — record a video that sweeps the settings through a range (presets, random, or your own saved stops).
- Export **MP4** (webm fallback) or **PNG**, and **save / load** your looks.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser (Chrome recommended for MP4 export), or visit the live Pages link above.

Made for [Precious FM](https://gutsyradio.org).
